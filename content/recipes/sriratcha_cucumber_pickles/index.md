These are refrigerator pickles, not needing a water bath or fermentation.

### Ingredients

- 1 English cucumber (or 4–5 Persian cucumbers)
- 1/4 C sriracha
- 1 C vinegar
- 1 T sesame oil
- 1 shallot
- 1 T sugar

### Steps

1. Slice the cucumber and shallot thinly, using a mandoline or sharp knife.
2. Mix the vinegar, oil, sugar and sriracha.
3. Place the cucumbers and shallots in alternating layers in a mason jar. Pour the brine over, and work out air bubbles by tapping or using a chopstick.
4. Place in the fridge for a minimum of 24 hours.

### Serving ideas

The pickle juice can actually act as the vinegar in an oil and vinegar dressing on a simple salad.
