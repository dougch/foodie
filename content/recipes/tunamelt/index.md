Pandemic comfort food for me.

<!-- more -->

### Ingredients

- 1 can tuna (5 oz)
- 1 large flour tortilla
- 1 T Greek yogurt or mayo
- dash of olive oil
- 1 T Parmesan cheese
- dash of za'atar
- pinch of salt
- 2–3 cremini mushrooms

### Steps

1. Chop the mushrooms, and combine them with the fish, yogurt or mayo, cheese, oil, salt and spices in a bowl.
2. Mix together and spread over half of the tortilla, folding it over. This is a healthy portion, and could easily make two smaller servings.
3. Sauté in olive oil over medium heat, 2–3 minutes a side, or until golden brown.

### Serving ideas

A local falafel shop near me sells a hot sauce called zhoug (aka zhug or shug), made with serrano peppers and cilantro, that pairs really well with this.
