Nothing terribly special about this chili, other than the lack of spice.

<!-- more -->

I scaled this up for a volunteer catering event to feed the staff of an elementary school. For 45 people it nearly maxed out my little Instant Pot when cooking the beans.

### Ingredients

- 560 g cooked or canned black beans (or {{ q(a="200", u="g") }} dry)
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic
- 500 g stew meat (or ground beef)
- 1 can crushed tomatoes (12–14 oz)
- 1.5 T chili powder
- 1 T oregano
- 1 T basil
- 1 T red wine vinegar
- 1 bay leaf (optional)
- diced bell, hatch or jalapeño peppers (optional)
- sour cream (optional)

### Steps

#### Beans

For dry beans, set up the Instant Pot with the beans, double their volume of water or stock, a clove of garlic and a bay leaf.
Set the Instant Pot for beans and cook for an hour.

If using canned beans, not much to do- just open the can (they aren't as flavorful though).

The liquid for either method should go into the final pot; it acts as a thickener.

#### Meat

In the Instant Pot (after removing the beans), put it on sauté and brown the meat.

If using stew meat, add {{ q(a="1", u="C") }} of beef stock, 1 clove of garlic and 1 bay leaf, and cook on 'meat' for 35 minutes.
For ground beef, just brown it.

#### Final assembly

Mix the beans and meat (minus the bay leaves) in a large pot, together with the remaining ingredients: tomatoes, garlic, spices, vinegar.
Cook down for about 30 minutes on a simmer, stirring occasionally to make sure it doesn't burn. A quick smash with a potato masher helps, but resist the urge to pull out the immersion blender.

Bell pepper, jalapeño and sour cream are all optional final condiments.
Corn bread or Fritos are also a great optional add-on.

### Yield

Makes 6 servings.
