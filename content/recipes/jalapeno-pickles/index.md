Pickled jalapeño rings for tacos, nachos and sandwiches.

<!-- more -->

These are sweet/hot pickles. A hot brine softens the peppers just enough while keeping some crunch. The original recipe called for parboiling, if you want less crunch.

### Ingredients

- 1 kg jalapeños
- 3 cloves garlic, smashed
- 250 ml apple cider vinegar
- Pinch of kosher salt
- 220 g turbinado or brown sugar
- 1 t dried oregano (optional)

### Equipment

- Canning pot
- 12 half-pint jars

### Steps

1. Slice the jalapeños into rings, using a mandoline or sharp knife. Wear gloves, and don't touch your eyes.
2. Pack the jalapeños and garlic into the jars.
3. In a small saucepan, bring the vinegar, water, salt, sugar and oregano to a boil, stirring until the salt and sugar dissolve.
4. Pour the hot brine over the jalapeños, and work out air bubbles by tapping the jar or using a chopstick. Top up with brine to cover.
5. Wipe the rims of the jars with a finger and put the lids on, finger tight.
6. Process in a [boiling water bath](@/canning.md) for 10 minutes to seal.

### Yield

12 half-pint jars.

### Variations

- Add a sliced carrot and some onion for taqueria-style escabeche.
- Seed the jalapeños for a milder pickle.
