A little texture and extra sweetness helps sell it. Try cream cheese instead of butter on top; it gives a tang for the foodies.

<!-- more -->

### Ingredients

- 3 1/4 C flour
- 2 t baking soda
- 1 t freshly ground nutmeg
- 2 C pumpkin
- 2/3 C water (only if using canned pumpkin)
- 1 C coconut oil
- handful of raisins or dark chocolate chips
- handful of old-fashioned rolled oats
- 1 t salt
- 1 t cinnamon
- 1 t grated ginger
- 2 C brown sugar
- 1 C white sugar
- 3 eggs

### Steps

1. For the pumpkin, quarter a pumpkin and scoop out the seeds and insides. Roast in a 375°F oven for an hour, or until it smells like pumpkin and a knife goes in easily.
2. Grease and flour 2 loaf pans. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
3. Cream the pumpkin, oil, sugar and eggs in a large bowl. Add the spices.
4. In another bowl, add the salt, baking soda and flour. Pour into the loaf pans and top with oats, raisins or chocolate chips.
5. Place in the oven and immediately lower the temperature to 300°F. Bake for an hour, or until brown and a knife comes out clean when inserted.

### Yield

2 loaves.
