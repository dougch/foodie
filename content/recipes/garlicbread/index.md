Served as a common side, it's crunchy, cheesy and garlicky.

<!-- more -->

### Ingredients

- regular wheat bread, baguette or premade pizza dough
- Parmesan cheese
- 2–3 cloves garlic
- 1 stick butter or ghee

### Steps

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Chop the garlic into small chunks. Melt the butter in a ramekin and let the garlic steep a few minutes in the hot butter.
3. If using pizza dough, cut it into palm-sized chunks and roll them out into long strands.
4. Line a cookie sheet with parchment or nonstick spray, then flatten the dough on the cookie sheet.
5. Spread the garlic butter over your carbohydrate of choice and sprinkle liberally with grated Parmesan. My family does not approve of sprinkling Parmesan on top, so I only do one for myself.
6. Bake for 10 minutes, or until the Parmesan just starts to brown.
