Granola

Pistachio and cranberry granola for topping yogurt.

sidecrunchybreakfast

My take on granola, usually just mixed 50/50 with plain Greek yogurt.

While we're not big cereal-with-milk eaters, treating this as a topping on other things can work.

Ingredients

  • 3 C old-fashioned rolled oats
  • 1/3 C chopped pistachios
  • 1 t salt
  • 1 t cinnamon
  • dusting of nutmeg
  • 1/2 t almond extract or good-quality vanilla
  • 1/2 C coconut oil
  • 1/4 C brown sugar or honey
  • 1 C dried cranberries

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 325°F.
  2. In a large bowl, mix the oats, nuts and dry spices.
  3. In a separate glass measuring cup, mix the oil and sugar. Heat until the fat has melted. Stir in the almond or vanilla flavoring, then pour over the oat mixture.
  4. Spread thin in a large casserole and bake for 20 minutes, or until golden brown.
  5. After cooling down, stir in the dried fruit.

Variations

Obviously, there is quite a bit of room for substitutions. Different nuts, dried fruit or extracts are all an option. Sugar levels can be decreased as well, but the fat helps bind and crisp the final result.
