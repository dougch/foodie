Pulled Pork

Cured, smoked pork shoulder, finished in the oven.

Americanmaindry

My spin on this, which will offend purists, is to finish off the meat in the oven.

We still prefer barbacoa to this recipe.

Ingredients

  • 4.5 kg pork shoulder (10 lb)
  • Himalayan pink salt
  • soy sauce
  • 100 ml apple cider vinegar
  • peppercorns
  • 3–4 bay leaves
  • paprika
  • chili powder
  • several cloves garlic, crushed

Equipment

  • Smoker

Prep ahead

Put the shoulder in a high-sided casserole. Rub it all over with pink salt, focusing on the sides without the fat cap.

Place it uncovered in the fridge for 7 days, flipping it over once halfway through. You may want to drain the liquid after 24 hours.

Place it in a large container or marinade bag and add 400 ml of an acid (usually apple cider vinegar), 200 ml of a salt solution (usually soy sauce), the herbs, and then water to cover. Make sure the meat is completely submerged, and put it back in the fridge for 24–48 hours.

Steps

Smoke for 4 hours with pecan chips at 250°F. Move to a covered container and add about 100 ml of water. Cook in the oven at 325°F for 4 more hours, always covered.

Once done, it will be falling apart. Remove the fat and bone and pull with forks.

Serving ideas

These make great tacos, sliders and a myriad of other dishes. I generally portion out about 400 g into vacuum seal bags for freezing, and only immediately eat the last bit that doesn't make the 400 g bar.
