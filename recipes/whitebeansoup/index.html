White Bean Soup

Smoky white bean soup made with pork neck bones.

Italianmainsaucy

We got some smoked pork neck bones from a friend, and I thought I'd take a crack at making soup with them.

The beans used were Great Northern beans, which are small enough to not be mealy. Navy beans or lentils would also work, but for lentils the second cook time would be much shorter (15 minutes for brown/green).

Ingredients

  • 450 g dry small white beans, Great Northern or navy (1 lb)
  • 500 g smoked pork bones (neck, hocks, etc.)
  • 2 L stock, pork preferred, but chicken or veg will work
  • 1 medium yellow onion
  • 1 small carrot
  • 2–3 celery stalks
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 2 bay leaves

Equipment

  • Instant Pot

Steps

  1. In your Instant Pot liner, over the stove, add some olive oil and sweat the chopped onion for a few minutes.
  2. Make a mirepoix: add the diced celery and carrot and cook a few more minutes.
  3. Add the garlic, stock, bay leaves and pork. If the stock doesn't add up to 2 L , be sure to fill the gap with water.
  4. Seal the Instant Pot and cook on the meat setting for 30 minutes.
  5. Open the Instant Pot and taste the broth. Depending on whether the pork was salted, you may need to add some salt.
  6. Add the beans, reseal and cook for 1 hour on the beans setting.

Variations

Some ideas to make this more interesting: diced jalapeños, kale, loads of black pepper, or chipotle hot sauce, all added at the end after the beans are cooked.

Serving ideas

Garlic bread would also help as a flavor and texture contrast.
